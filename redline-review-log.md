5. Trustee discretion wording replaced lines 93-94:

### Exhibit D - Spousal Rights Clarification

9. Edit 5, surviving spouse termination/reabsorption, replaced lines 74-76 and added after line 87:
   Proposed language clarifies that a surviving spouse's interest ends upon death, inability to use the Camp Property, voluntary relinquishment, or no longer participating. Added language says the interest shall not pass to the surviving spouse's heirs, estate, later spouse, or any non-descendant of the original beneficiary.

10. Edit 6, same-branch-first redistribution, added after line 70:
    If an interest would pass to multiple descendants of an original beneficiary and one descendant declines, relinquishes, or cannot/will not participate, that share first redistributes among the remaining participating descendants of the same original beneficiary's branch. If no descendant of that branch participates, it redistributes equally among remaining active beneficiary branches.
